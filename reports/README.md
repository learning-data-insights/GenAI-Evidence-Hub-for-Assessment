# Reports

This folder holds the reports produced by the GenAI Evidence Hub for Assessment. It also links to related papers published elsewhere. Unless a file says otherwise, reports in this folder are released under CC BY 4.0.

## Related papers (published elsewhere)

**Practitioner report.** Practical insights for edtech product teams, procurement and implementation staff, educators, and families. Summarizes what a structured review of 250 studies found about how AI is evaluated in automated scoring, item generation, and formative feedback.
Andres, A., Kurimchak, M., Burkhardt, A., Beiting-Parrish, M., & Feldstein, M. (2026, November 12). *The standard of proof for AI in EdTech: Applying measurement science to AI use in education assessments.* 1EdTech. https://www.1edtech.org/sites/default/files/media/docs/2026/1edtechldinsightsaiinedtechreport.pdf

**triageQ white paper.** Describes the human-in-the-loop LLM tool the team used to screen candidate papers against the study's selection criteria.
Kurimchak, M., Whitmer, J., Li, W., Beiting-Parrish, M., & Choi, H. (2026). *triageQ: A human-in-the-loop automated tool to select research papers meeting complex criteria.* Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23735664

**IRAISE short paper (Festival of Learning 2026).** Early findings from the Automated Scoring domain.
Li, W., Kurimchak, M., Andres, A., & Whitmer, J. (2026). *Evaluating the landscape of automated scoring with GenAI: A systematic review.* EdArXiv. https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/2uwcp_v1

**IRR Calculator Tool white paper.** Forthcoming. Describes the tool the team used to compute inter-rater reliability and find disagreements before reconciliation.
